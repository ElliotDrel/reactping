How ReactPing protects your messages

No tracking, and your messages are never stored. You don't have to take our word for it: ReactPing is open source, and every claim below links to the exact code.

Two permissions

ReactPing asks Slack to see reactions and to send you a DM. Nothing else.

See the permissions

Reactions only

No other data is needed. When someone reacts to your message, ReactPing checks it's someone else's reaction to your message in the same workspace, then sends your DM.

See the event handling See every Slack call it makes

Minimal storage

One database table: your Slack workspace and user IDs, the workspace URL, and encrypted access tokens.

See the table

Encrypted tokens

Slack access tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they're saved.

See the encryption

No tracking

No analytics, no scripts, and no cookies except one short-lived cookie that secures adding ReactPing to Slack. The site's security policy blocks scripts outright.

See the policy

Leave any time

Select Disconnect in ReactPing's Home tab in Slack and your record is deleted. Full details are in the privacy policy.

Read the privacy policy
