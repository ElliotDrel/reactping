Before you continue

  1. Pick the right workspace. On Slack's approval page, check the workspace name in the top-right corner and switch it if needed.
  2. Select Allow. ReactPing asks for two permissions: see reactions and send you a DM.
  3. Repeat for each workspace where you want notifications.

Everyone connects for themselves; teammates can share this page. If your workspace requires admin approval, Slack lets you request it. Connect once it's approved.
