Questions

Do my own reactions notify me?
No. ReactPing ignores reactions you add to your own messages, so you only hear about other people.
Does everyone need to add it?
Only people who want DMs. Everyone adds it for themselves.
What if my admin approves apps?
Slack lets you request approval. Add ReactPing again once it's approved.
How do I disconnect?
Open ReactPing in Slack, go to Home, and select Disconnect. Your record is deleted.
