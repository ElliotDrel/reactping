How it works

Add it once, then it stays out of your way.

  1. 1

    Add to Slack

    Approve two narrow permissions. Your Slack account is your login; there's nothing else to sign up for.

  2. 2

    Get a welcome DM

    ReactPing confirms it can reach you. Its Home tab in Slack shows your status and a Disconnect button.

  3. 3

    Get a DM per reaction

    When someone else reacts to your message, ReactPing tells you who, which emoji, and where.

Good to know

Small, focused, and honest about its limits.

One workspace at a time

Notifications arrive in the same workspace where the reaction happened. Add ReactPing separately in each workspace you use.

Best effort, no backfill

If Slack can't reach ReactPing or a DM fails, that notification is skipped. Reactions from before you connected, or during an outage, are never sent later.

Easy to leave

Press Disconnect in ReactPing's Home tab, or remove the app in Slack. Your stored tokens are deleted right away.

Privacy by design

Two permissions. No message text.

ReactPing asks for the least Slack access that can do the job. It never reads what you wrote, never downloads history, and keeps no record of your reactions: each one is turned into a DM and forgotten.

Questions

Short answers

Which reactions trigger a DM?

Reactions other people add to messages you wrote, in a workspace where you added ReactPing. Reactions on other people's messages, on files, removed reactions, and your own reactions to your own messages never send anything.

Can I get notifications from several workspaces in one place?

Not yet. Each workspace's notifications stay in that workspace. A single inbox across workspaces may come later.

Which conversations are covered?

Messages in conversations you can see. Live testing of this version is still in progress; public channels were confirmed on an earlier version, and private channels, threads, DMs, group DMs, and Slack Connect channels have not been confirmed yet.

My workspace needs admin approval for apps. What happens?

Slack shows a request form instead of the approval screen. Once an admin approves, add ReactPing again. Enterprise Grid organizations are not supported.

Is it free?

ReactPing is a free pilot with no uptime commitment; it may change, pause, or end.
