How ReactPing works

In a busy Slack channel, reactions to your messages are easy to miss. ReactPing sends you a DM each time someone reacts, so you know who responded and can jump straight back.

1. Add it to Slack

Select Add to Slack, pick your workspace, and select Allow. ReactPing sends a welcome DM so you know it's working. Use more than one workspace? Add it to each one.

2. Someone reacts to your message

Any emoji reaction from someone else on a message you wrote triggers a DM. Your own reactions don't. Each person adds ReactPing for themselves, so teammates who haven't added it won't get DMs.

3. You get a DM

The DM shows who reacted, the emoji, and a short preview of your message. "Your message" links straight back to it. The DM arrives in the same workspace where the reaction happened.

Good to know

  • Notifications are best effort. A reaction missed during an outage isn't replayed.
  • If your workspace requires admin approval for apps, Slack lets you request it. Add ReactPing again once it's approved.
  • To stop, open ReactPing in Slack, go to Home, and select Disconnect.

Built to respect your privacy

Two permissions, no tracking, and your messages are never stored. Every claim links to the open-source code.
