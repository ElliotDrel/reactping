Privacy

Your data. Kept simple.

What we store, why we need it, and how you remove it.

What we store

Your Slack workspace and user IDs, workspace URL, encrypted access tokens, and connection timestamps. These let ReactPing recognize your connection and send you DMs.

What we don't store

Message text, files, channel history, names, email addresses, or reaction history. Reaction details are processed to send your notification, then discarded. The notification itself stays in Slack.

How we use it

To deliver reaction notifications and manage your connection. Notifications stay in the workspace where the reaction happened. No advertising, tracking, or sale of your data.

Security and cookies

Access tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. During installation, one signed, HttpOnly cookie connects Slack's approval to your browser. It expires after 10 minutes and is cleared when you return. The website has no tracking scripts.

Our service providers

Vercel hosts the website and API. Supabase stores connection records. Slack supplies reaction events and delivers DMs. These providers process data to run the service and may keep operational logs such as IP addresses and request times.

ReactPing's logs contain workspace IDs and error codes, never access tokens, authorization codes, or message content.

Disconnect and delete

Select Disconnect in ReactPing's Slack Home tab to delete your connection record and request revocation of your user token. Other people's connections stay active. Removing ReactPing from a workspace deletes its connection records when Slack notifies us. Revocation events remove the affected connections.

Delivery

Notifications are best effort. Failed deliveries and reactions during downtime aren't replayed. Our free-tier database may pause after about a week of low activity; notifications resume after the project is restored.

Questions?

Contact the person who shared ReactPing with you for help with your connection or data.
